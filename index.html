    Notes
      How to read this
      Chance
      The model's chance this player scores the game's first basket. A random pick would be 10%.
      Fair odds
      The break-even price. A bet only makes sense if the sportsbook pays more (e.g. +700 when fair is +500).
      DraftKings (FG only)
      DraftKings' price for its first-scorer bet. DraftKings only counts the first field goal — free throws don't count (FanDuel's do). So we judge DraftKings prices with a separate field-goal chance for each player, and FanDuel's with the normal chance. The ★ goes to whichever is the better deal.
      FanDuel
      FanDuel's current First Basket price. Green = it pays more than the fair odds, so the model sees value. Grey = it pays less.
      Value (EV)
      Is FanDuel's prize big enough for how often the model thinks he scores first? Green +10¢ = good deal: about 10¢ profit for every $1 over many bets. Grey = bad deal. A good deal still loses most nights.
      ↓ ↑ under a price
      ↓ = the payout dropped since this morning: the sportsbook now thinks he's more likely to score first (people are betting him). ↑ = it went up. If one of our good bets shows ↓, it's getting worse — bet it soon (shown as ⏰ in Tonight's best bets).
      Best deal
      The best-value player in the game (out of all 10 starters, not just the top 5).
      Tip-off
      Who jumps for each team (the player who usually takes the tip, or NBA.com's listed centre) and each one's chance to win the opening jump ball; the favourite is in bold. The team that wins the tip scores first about 66% of the time. "Not known yet" = no tip history for that team's starters.
      Scores first
      Each team's chance to score the game's first points.
      Lineup
      Confirmed = official NBA.com starters. Expected = NBA.com's expected starters, can change. Projected = our own guess; NBA.com usually posts lineups in the early afternoon (ET).
      Tonight
      Live scores refresh every minute. Once a game starts you'll see who scored first and whether it was our pick.
